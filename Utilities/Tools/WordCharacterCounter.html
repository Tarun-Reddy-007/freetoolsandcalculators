About Word and Character Counting

Word and character counts are useful when a document has a length requirement, a form limits input, or a piece of writing needs a quick readability check. This tool updates as you type or paste text and separates words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, and lines so you can see how different editing choices affect the total.

Counting conventions vary between applications. Whitespace, punctuation, line breaks, abbreviations, symbols, emoji, and languages that do not use spaces can all produce different results. Use this counter for a consistent local reference, then check the destination platform when its limit or parsing rules are especially strict. The entered text is not needed by the calculator after the page is closed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter define a word?

It identifies word-like groups using the browser’s regular-expression rules and counts them after trimming the text. Hyphenation, symbols, and languages with different writing systems may be interpreted differently by other applications.

Are spaces included in the character count?

Yes. The tool reports characters with spaces and separately reports characters after whitespace is removed, making both common publishing conventions easy to compare.

Why can sentence counts be different from my editor?

Sentence detection is an estimate based on punctuation and nearby characters. Abbreviations, decimal points, headings, and unusual punctuation can cause a text editor or publishing platform to count differently.

Is my text stored or uploaded?

Counting occurs in the browser, so the tool does not need to upload the text for its calculations. Avoid entering sensitive material into any website unless its privacy practices meet your requirements.
